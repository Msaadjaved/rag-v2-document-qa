## 5. BM25

- **Index:** own inverted index (`app/bm25.py`), k1 = 1.5, b = 0.75, persisted as JSON in `data/index/`, one file per corpus, chunking and tokenizer variant; written atomically. (D-03, D-04)
- **Tokenization:** lowercase, accent folding, hyphenated identifiers indexed whole and split (`KST-5031` → `kst-5031`, `kst`, `5031`), English stop-word list. Stop-word ablation (BM25 alone, Hit@1: Kestrel 0.62 vs 0.38, own 0.75 vs 0.58; one Kestrel question loses at k = 3). (D-05)
- **Updates:** a fingerprint of chunk texts + tokenizer settings is stored in the file; upload, delete, selection or chunking changes rebuild the index on the next query; no incremental update, rebuild ≈ 10 ms at this size. Tested in `tests/test_bm25.py`. (D-04)
